    Metric definitions
    Visit
    One focus on a field, ending at the next blur of that field.
    Time on field
    Blur time minus focus time. Visits without a blur, with a negative interval, or longer than {{ max_duration_min }} minutes are excluded from time metrics but still count as visits.
    Error rate
    Visits with at least one validation error ÷ visits × 100.
    Correction rate
    Visits with at least one correction (an edit after an error) ÷ visits × 100.
    Progression
    Visits after which the session focused a later field or submitted the form ÷ visits × 100.
    Drop-off
    Abandoned sessions whose last visited field was this one ÷ sessions that visited this field × 100.
    Session status
    Completed if the form was completed; abandoned if idle for {{ abandon_window_min }} minutes without completing; otherwise in progress.
    Abandonment rate
    Abandoned ÷ (completed + abandoned) × 100. In-progress sessions are excluded because their outcome is not yet known.
    N/A
    No observations exist to calculate the value. It is never shown as 0.
